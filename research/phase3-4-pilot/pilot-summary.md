## Item decisions

- PLOS ONE, “First evidence of microplastic inhalation among free-ranging small cetaceans” (DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0309377`): the individual article page states CC BY 4.0. The abstract is retained with citation attribution. Figure 1's map names Esri, TomTom, Garmin, FAO, NOAA, USGS, OpenStreetMap and the GIS User Community; the map and all other media are excluded because their individual asset rights were not cleared.
- PLOS ONE, “Conceptions of university students on microplastics in Germany” (DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0257734`): the individual article page states CC BY 4.0. The abstract is retained with citation attribution. No figures, tables, supplementary files, or participant-level data are retained; rights for individual assets were not reviewed.
- Six VOA Learning English items: title, RSS link/GUID and published date were observed in the official RSS feed. The feed had no author values for these items. Their canonical HTML URLs were not independently inspected, so the RSS links are explicitly marked as uncanonicalized. Item-level exclusive VOA authorship is unresolved, so only metadata and links are retained; no article bodies were fetched.

## Quality and unresolved items

- Required titles and source URLs are present for all eight records. PLOS DOI values are unique; VOA GUIDs are unique. The deterministic validator checks duplicate canonical URLs and DOI values.
- VOA author, page canonical URL, content type, subject categories, and geographic focus remain unassigned because the metadata-only acquisition did not establish them.
- CEFR levels are not assigned.
- PLOS article-level reuse is supported by each individual page's copyright statement, not only the general journal policy. The PLOS source registry entry remains at its Phase 3.3 `unknown` / `link-only` baseline; the pilot's item exception is isolated and does not change production policy.
- The source-level rights evidence is not a legal determination. Re-check the item page and all third-party assets before any later republication.
